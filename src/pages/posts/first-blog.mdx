I also want to put in equation here to test if it's working (*so I guess this is sort of a tutorial and not an authentic piece of literature*). Here's the Schrodinger equation:

$$
\hat{H}\ket{\Psi} = i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\ket{\Psi}
$$

Now let me do something extremely controversial because I rule this domain like that yk:

$$
\hat{H}\cancel{\ket{\Psi}} = i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\cancel{\ket{\Psi}} \implies \hat{H} = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}i\hbar.
$$

But, the $ \:\^{}\: $ is trivial and I don't like the $ \frac{\partial}{\partial t} $ (for absolutely no reason at all), so:

$$
H = i\hbar.
$$
