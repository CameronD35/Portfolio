This was the absolute **highlight** of my time at College of the Canyons. Going into Tutoring, I was a nervous wreck. Though I has helped my sister with primary and secondary school mathematics. I was so afraid I wouldn't be a good tutor. Little did I know, that I would absolutely fall in love with tutoring and teaching so much that it became *my thing*. Everytime I walked into the MESA center I knew what I was getting my into: another tutoring session. But did I regret it? No!!! 

While working as a tutor, I have never felt more fulfilled. Since then, I've set my sights on becoming a physics or mathematics professor. This was furthered by the opportunity I got to make my own worksheets (and even a secret MESA Tutor Evaluation!) with my own math and physics problems for Academic Excellence Workshosp (AEWs) for Linear Algebra and Physics: Electricity & Magnetism. I'll admit, since this was my first time creating problems, some of them were really messy (sorry to the Linear Algebra students who were patience with me as the problems I created unfolded into the ugliest numbers ever). But creating and solving them became a stepping stone in understanding the material better and teaching it as though I was already a professor!

Below are just some of the achievements I have been so honored to be a part of:

* Received the Dr. Ram Manvi Academic Excellence Award for maintaining a 4.0 GPA while providing outstanding tutoring

* Helped 20+ students across Linear Algebra, Calculus, Calculus-Based Physics, Computer Science, and Chemistry

* Led Mathematics and Physics Academic Excellence Workshops and crafted unique problems to solve with students
